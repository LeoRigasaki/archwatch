Facts come from config.json, safetensors headers, modeling code and model cards at pinned commits. A fact is verified when two independent sources agree; a fact with one source is labelled as such, and a disagreement is held for a person to resolve. Cache sizes are computed by ArchWatch's calculator from published fields. Diagrams and explanations are written by Gemini from verified fields only, and every number they contain is checked by code.
