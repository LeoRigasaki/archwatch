Groups: **ours_wrong** = a confirmed bug in our code (or weights we did not read); **his_wrong** = the model's own files contradict his value; **card_unreproducible** = we read every tensor and no fixed or card-stated rule reproduces the card's figure (what we checked is listed); **name_rounding** = his value is the size in the model name or a card '~' figure, and our exact count is within half a unit of its last digit; **definition** = our number under the fixed rule (or a rule the card states) lands in his accepted range; **unresolved** = review queue.

Fixed rules (the same for every model without a card statement): `params_total` = all stored tensors minus MTP modules (reported separately) and minus vision tower and projector (text-only figure); `params_active` = per-token count; unselected experts, MTP, lookup tables and the input embedding (row lookups) not active; output head counted (tied: shared matrix once, as the head); `kv_cache_per_token_bf16` = full retention, every attention layer appends its KV per token (sliding-window layers not capped). An exclusion (embedding, output head, MTP, lookup tables) or a vision inclusion is applied only when the card sentence that states his figure says so; that sentence is quoted below. Accepted range: 2% or the card's own rounding.
